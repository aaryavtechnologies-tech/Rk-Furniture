The Art of Wood Carving

Saharanpur's renowned wood carving heritage, highest quality craftsmanship by renowned wood carving artisan masters. We do not use CNC machines for structural carving — every curve, crown, and floral motif is carved by hand with steel chisels.

1

Grade-A Seasoned Timber Selection

2

Master Hand-Chiseling & Joinery

Absolutely. As genuine direct manufacturers, every piece can be tailored to your room dimensions, floorplan CAD drawings, or architect specifications. You can select your preferred fabric (royal velvet, jacquard, leatherette), cushion density, and wood finish (24K Antique Gold Leaf, Natural Honey Teak, Imperial Walnut, or Dual-Tone White & Gold).

We exclusively utilize 100% kiln-dried seasoned Grade-A Teak Wood (Sagwan) and Sheesham (Indian Rosewood). The timber is aged to controlled moisture content (below 12%) and treated with specialized anti-borer and anti-termite chemical immersion, ensuring zero warping, cracking, or pest infestation for decades.

Once we finalize your dimensions and finish specifications, an initial 30% advance deposit confirms your raw timber allocation and carving schedule. You receive milestone video updates during carving, and the remaining balance is paid upon final video inspection right before dispatch.
